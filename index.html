Important limitations

Designed for experiments, not navigation.

What it tries to do

Name remembered faces and count other people, label common objects with their direction and rough closeness, flag approaching vehicles, note stop signs and traffic-light colour, and read printed text it sees.

What it cannot guarantee

It can miss obstacles, vehicles, kerbs, stairs and holes. Distance is only a rough guess from object size. Traffic-light colour and text reading can be wrong, especially in glare, darkness or motion. It never tells you it is safe to cross. Keep using your cane, guide dog or trained assistance.
